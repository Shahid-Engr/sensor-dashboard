            Everything on this console is also available through a REST API, so other systems can read data, onboard devices and change limits. Devices publish to MQTT topics such as fieldmesh/<site>/<device>/telemetry.

            Endpoints

            • GET/api/v1/devicesList devices with status and firmware
            • POST/api/v1/devicesOnboard a Wi-Fi or LoRaWAN device
            • GET/api/v1/devices/{id}/telemetry?from=&to=Readings for a time range
            • PUT/api/v1/devices/{id}/configChange reporting interval and settings
            • POST/api/v1/devices/{id}/otaStart a firmware update
            • PUT/api/v1/devices/{id}/thresholdsSet low and high limits
            • GET/api/v1/alarms?status=activeOpen alarms
            • GET/api/v1/camera/eventsAI detection events
